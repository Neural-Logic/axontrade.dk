The parent company

The company behind Neural Logic and Hockey24.

Axon Trade ApS is a Danish private limited company in Copenhagen, founded in September 2025 and run by its founder and sole owner. It operates two ventures under their own names. Whatever you sign with either of them, the counterparty is this company: it issues the invoice, holds the data and carries the responsibility.

What a counterparty gets.

Most people who read this page are about to sign something, or to clear a signature for someone else. This is what they need to know.

  1. One legal entity.Contracts, invoices, data-processing agreements and any certificate of insurance name Axon Trade ApS and register number 45 92 07 63. There is no second company behind the brands.
  2. A public record.The company is registered with the Danish Business Authority. The register extract is public, and the facts on this page are taken from it.
  3. One signature.The company is signed for by its director, Thomas Möller, who founded it and owns it in full.
  4. European processing.Client data in Neural Logic work stays in Europe, under a data-processing agreement, with the roles written down before any document is read.
